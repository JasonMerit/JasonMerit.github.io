a) What is the difference between Phong shading and Phong lighting (the Phong reflection model)?

- Phong lighting is a model used to color the surface of objects based on the perspective of the viewer, light sources, and material properties. It is divided into three components: Ambient, Diffuse, and Specular.
- Phong shading is applying the lighting to each pixel. This amounts to computing the color in the fragment shader instead of the vertex shader, where it is only done on a vertex basis. The fragments automatically interpolate the colors between vertices, so it is necessary to renormalize.
- The difference is that the first gives the expression for the color, while the latter applies it.

b) What is the difference between flat shading, Gouraud shading, and Phong shading? List pros and cons of each. Is Gouraud or Phong shading the best method for simulating highlights? Explain.

- Going down the list, the computation becomes heavier, but the results appear more realistic.
Flat shading: Entire shape is colored the same color, making it appear flat.
  Pros: No computation required.
  Cons: Flat appearance.

Gouraud shading: Lighting is computed for each vertex.
  Pros: Smoother shading.
  Cons: Details may be missed for simpler polygons, and the specular highlight can be inaccurate.

Phong shading: Lighting is computed for each pixel.
  Pros: Perfectly smooth.
  Cons: Computationally expensive.

For highlighting, Phong shading is evidently the best. This is due to highlighting occurring between vertices and the distribution being non-linear.

c) What is the difference between a directional light and a point light?

- Directional light assumes the source is at an infinite distance that produces parallel rays.
- Point light emits from a point in space, and the intensity decreases over distance.
